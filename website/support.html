Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email tend-us.support@proton.me. Please include your device model and iOS version.

How do I play?

Drag your paddle with your finger. Hit the puck into the goal at the far end of the table and defend your own. The first player to reach the target score (5, 7 or 11, set in the menu) wins.

How does Career work?

Career has 12 leagues of 8 stages. Win a stage to unlock the next one. The last stage of every league is a boss with special rules, such as pinball bumpers, a barrier across the goal, wind, a blackout, or flipped controls. The rules are shown before each match starts. Beat the final boss, OMEGA, to open the endless Infinity Tower.

How does Endless work?

Endless is one long game. You start with 3 lives and lose one each time the CPU scores. Every goal you score makes the CPU a little better, every 5 points the rules change at random, and every 10 points you earn a bonus life (up to 5). Your best score is shown on the menu.

How do stars and unlocks work?

Each win earns 1 to 3 stars: the fewer goals you let in, the more stars you get, and you can replay any cleared stage to improve. Your star total sets your rank. Every boss you beat, and every star milestone, unlocks a new paddle or table, which you can choose in the Style section of the menu. Tap a locked one to see how to earn it.

How do I play with a friend?

Tap 2 Players, lay your phone or iPad flat between you, and each take one end. Both players can touch the screen at the same time.

How do I pause?

Tap the round pause button on the left edge of the centre line. The game also pauses by itself if you leave the app or get a call.

There's no sound

The game respects your iPhone's silent switch, so check it's not on silent. Also check that Sound is turned on at the bottom of the menu.

How do I reset my progress?

Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets everything, including your Career and unlocked colours.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not currently. Progress isn't backed up to any server, because the game collects no data. See the privacy policy.
