Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Neon Air Hockey: Boss Rush ("the game") is made by Recluze. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the game. The short version: the game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No accounts, sign-in, or user profiles.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • The game makes no network requests and works fully offline.
  • The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or any other device permission.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, vibration, difficulty, match length, chosen paddle and table colours), your Career progress and your win/loss record are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

Ratings prompt

After a few wins the game may show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt. That prompt is provided and handled entirely by Apple; the game never sees whether or how you rate it.

Apple

Downloading the game from the App Store is subject to Apple's own privacy policy, which covers information Apple collects about purchases and downloads. We do not receive personal information from Apple about you.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
