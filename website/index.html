NeonAir Hockey

Arcade air hockey for iPhone and iPad. Drag your paddle, slam the puck, and work your way up to a final boss that cheats.

Get it on the App Store Coming soon.

Career

96 stages across 12 leagues. Every league ends in a boss that changes the rules: pinball bumpers, a barrier sliding across the goal, wind, curving shots, a blackout lit by lightning, a king who flips your controls. Beat the last one and an endless tower opens up.

Endless

Three lives. The CPU gets sharper with every goal you score, and the rules shuffle every five points.

Two players

Lay your phone or iPad flat and play face to face, one end each, both hands on the glass.

No ads, no accounts

It's free, works offline and collects no data. Stars and bosses unlock 28 paddles and tables.
