# Neon Air Hockey — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

_The published version of this policy is `website/privacy.html`; keep the two in step._

Neon Air Hockey does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- No accounts, sign-in, or user profiles.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- No network requests are made by the game.
- Your settings (sound, vibration, difficulty, match length, chosen colours),
  your Career progress and your win/loss record are saved **only on your
  device** and are deleted when you delete the app.

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, location,
contacts, photos, or any other device permission.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone,
including children under 13.
